FieldSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

FieldSense is an Android app with a compass, bubble level, speedometer and altimeter. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it.

Summary

FieldSense does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's precise location (GPS). It uses it only on your phone to:

  • correct the compass for magnetic declination and show your distance to the North Pole,
  • calculate speed, distance and moving time,
  • show your altitude and calibrate the barometric altimeter.

Your location is kept in memory only while the app is running. It is never saved to storage and never sent off your device. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings; features that do not need location keep working without it.

Motion and pressure sensors

The app reads the rotation, magnetic field, gyroscope and pressure sensors to show its instruments. These readings stay on your device and are not stored or transmitted.

Advertising

The current version of FieldSense shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what data the ad provider receives and how to control it.

Children

FieldSense is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
